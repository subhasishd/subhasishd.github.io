About Research Teaching

About

I am a Professor of Economics at the University of Utah and an affiliated faculty member of the University's Asia Center. I study how people behave in markets, contests, and bargaining, using laboratory experiments and field experiments, many of them in India. My work spans psychology and economics, applied game theory, and applied microeconomic theory.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Arizona in 2006 and taught at the University of Calgary before joining Utah in 2016.
