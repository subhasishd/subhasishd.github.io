My name is Subhasish Dugar, and I am a Professor in the Economics department at the University of Utah. Previously, I was as an assistant and associate professor (with tenure) in the Department of Economics at the University of Calgary, Canada. Prior to that, I completed my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Arizona. 

My work is at the intersection of Economics and Psychology. I use neoclassical tools and economics experiments (lab, field, and online) to study human behavior in strategic settings. I am also a member of this lab, ULEEF.

Sometimes people ask how to pronounce my first name, so here is an attempt at phonetics: Subhasish, is pronounced as "shoe-va-sish."

I acknowledge that I live and work on the ancestral & traditional lands of the Goshute, Paiute, Shoshone, and Ute peoples.
